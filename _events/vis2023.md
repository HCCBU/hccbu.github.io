This week the group headed all the way over to Australia for the first ever IEE VIS2023 in the area, the conferance was held at the melbourne Convention and Exhibition Center, and what an experience it has been! 

We were proud to present our work on **Wizualization** (our “hard magic” immersive analytics system), but what really made the trip memorable was the people, the conversations, and the shared excitement around where visualization is heading.

## What made this VIS memorable

- Meeting friends and collaborators from across the VIS community
- Seeing so many creative ideas around XR, interaction, and visual storytelling
- Having thoughtful discussions after talks, posters, and demos
- Taking time as a team to reflect, recharge, and get inspired for what comes next

The conference itself had a great atmosphere, and being in **Melbourne** gave this edition a unique energy. VIS 2023 was also the first VIS hosted in the Southern Hemisphere, which made it feel like a landmark year for the community.

## A team moment

For us, this was not just about a publication milestone. It was a reminder of why we do this work: building ideas together, learning from others, and contributing to a welcoming research community.

We came back with fresh motivation, new connections, and plenty of ideas for future HCC projects.
