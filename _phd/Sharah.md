Sarah is currently a PhD student in the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Bangor University, UK. She gained her first degree in Visual Art and worked as a graphic designer for many years, after which she ran a small hospitality business. After developing an interest in design for the web she crossed disciplines to STEM via an MSc in Computing. Her research focuses on how medical education and training may be supported by interactive visualization in 3D and augmented reality using collaborative web-based solutions. Her work is supported by the AIMLAC programme, funded by UKRI.
