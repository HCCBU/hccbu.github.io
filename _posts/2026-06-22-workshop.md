Pete and Panos co-organised, along with [Dr Ken Valyear](https://www.bangor.ac.uk/staff/spss/ken-valyear-106266/en), from the [Hand-and-Brain lab](https://sites.google.com/site/kfvalyear/bio-sketch?authuser=0), [School of Psychology and Sport Science](https://www.bangor.ac.uk/spss), [Bangor University](https://www.bangor.ac.uk/) three workshops on Human-computer Interaction-enabled stroke rehabilitation, between the 16th and the 22nd of June.

The workshops brought together clinicians, from [Ysbyty Eriri](https://bcuhb.nhs.wales/patients-and-visitors/hospitals/ysbyty-eryri-caernarfon/) and [Deeside Community Hospital](https://bcuhb.nhs.wales/patients-and-visitors/hospitals/deeside-community-hospital/), working on stroke rehabilitations, along with Neuroscientist, Sports Scientists, Engineers and Computer Scientists, to discuss how HCI flavors, such as XR and Human-Centred AI (HCAI), can provide opportunities for more effective, patient-centric services. The last day had a presentation from [Dr Gavin Buckingham](https://experts.exeter.ac.uk/25293-gavin-buckingham) from the University of Exeter.

The workshops were funded by the Wales Research Environment and Culture (WREC) cross-disciplinary seed-corn fund.
