Panos presented in [EG EuroVis 2026](https://eurovis.org.uk/), the paper ["Preparedness for visualization in the next pandemic"]({{site.baseurl}}/publicationsYear#McGee-et-al-CGA-2025), published in [_IEEE Computer Graphics and Applications_](https://www.computer.org/csdl/magazine/cg), and featured in [_IEEE Computing Edge, in November 2025_](https://ieeecs-media.computer.org/media/marketing/cedge_digital/ce-nov25-final.pdf). 


This article discusses considerations on how visualization can be best positioned to help response to future pandemics. The work is a product of the Dagstuhl Seminar 23482 on "Visualization for Pandemic Preparedness" held in January 2024. You can find more info on the seminar [here](https://www.dagstuhl.de/en/program/calendar/semhp/?semnr=23482).
