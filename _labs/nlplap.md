The Natural Language Processing (NLP) Lab focuses on computational methods that enable machines to understand and generate human language. Its research covers text processing, linguistic modelling, and intelligent interfaces that support communication and analysis. Through interdisciplinary work, the lab develops language driven technologies for education, accessibility, and information retrieval.
