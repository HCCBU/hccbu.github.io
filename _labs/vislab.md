The Visualization and Analytics (VIS) Lab, led by Professor Jonathan C. Roberts, investigates how people understand complex information through visual and interactive techniques. The lab focuses on information visualization, visual analytics, exploratory interfaces, multiple coordinated views, and innovative sensory modalities such as tangible and touch‑based interaction. Its work spans Big Data, multivariate analysis, and collaborations with climate scientists, language interpreters, social scientists, ocean researchers, and heritage specialists.
