The XReality and Visual Analytics (XRVA) Lab brings together expertise in human‑computer interaction, immersive technologies, and next‑generation visual analytics. Led by Dr Panagiotis Ritos and supported by Dr Peter Butcher, the lab investigates how mixed reality, virtual reality, and cross‑device ecosystems can enhance human‑data interaction. Their work explores immersive analytics, beyond‑the‑desktop visualization, and situated data experiences, with a strong emphasis on Web‑based interactive and collaborative visual analytics systems.
