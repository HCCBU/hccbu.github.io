Glory is a PhD student at the [School of Computer Science and Engineering](http://www.cs.bangor.ac.uk/), [Bangor University, UK](http://www.bangor.ac.uk/) focused on AI-assisted legal guidance and process visualization. His work combines legal technologies with generative AI, human-centric design, and information visualisation. Glory's work is supported by the [AIMLAC](https://cdt-aimlac.org/) programme, funded by UKRI. [More info](https://www.bangor.ac.uk/research-students/scse/glory-ogbonda-594600/en).,
