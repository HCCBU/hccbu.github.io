Rafi is a PhD student in the [School of Computer Science and Engineering](http://www.cs.bangor.ac.uk/), [Bangor University, UK](http://www.bangor.ac.uk/). His research explores how Large Language Models and multi agent AI systems can assist students and novice designers in applying the Five Design Sheets methodology. He is particularly interested in AI assisted design tools, prompt engineering, and making structured design methods more accessible in educational settings. [More info](https://www.bangor.ac.uk/research-students/scse/).
