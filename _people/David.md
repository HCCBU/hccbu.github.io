David is a MScRes student in the [School of Computer Science and Engineering](http://www.cs.bangor.ac.uk/), [Bangor University, UK](http://www.bangor.ac.uk/) His research focusses on visualising large datasets in immersive spaces. He is currently working towards making 3D datasets easier to understand in collaborative environment, by reducing visual clutter and focussing on displaying contextually appropriate information, while maintaining shared awareness between collaborators during information exploration.
