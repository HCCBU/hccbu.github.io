Welcome Sioned to HCC

Team Update February 17, 2026

We are thrilled to announce that Sioned Owen has joined the Human-Centred Computing research group as an undergraduate intern through Bangor University’s internship programme!

About Sioned’s Role

Sioned is bringing fresh perspectives and creative energy to HCC as she works on an exciting project focused on branding and redesigning the HCC research group website. Her internship combines her passion for design with our commitment to enhancing how we present our research and culture to the world.

What to Expect

Over the coming months, Sioned will be working on:

  • Visual Identity: Developing and refining the visual branding for HCC
  • Website Redesign: Creating a more engaging and user-friendly web presence
  • Design Strategy: Collaborating with the team to align design with our research values and mission

Join Us in Welcoming Sioned

Her fresh perspective and design expertise are already making an impact on our digital presence. We look forward to seeing the innovative ideas and creative solutions she brings to HCC.

Welcome to the team, Sioned!

Sioned is part of Bangor University’s undergraduate internship programme, providing students with valuable real-world experience in their field of study.
